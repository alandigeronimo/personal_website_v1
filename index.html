01 / THE STORY

A little about me.

My Roots

I was born and raised in Montclair, NJ. My dad is an investment professional, and my mom was a teacher before staying home to raise my younger brother and me. Growing up, I spent summers exploring the outdoors with my family in Vermont, enjoyed the methodical process of folding intricate origami and building elaborate LEGO creations, and channeled my competitive energy into ice hockey. I've always loved working through complex challenges to achieve tangible results, one step at a time.

The Road So Far

My entrepreneurial spirit emerged in high school when I began reselling limited-edition sneakers and apparel for profit. In college, I majored in finance and helped launch a beverage startup. After graduating, I spent two years as an investment banker, developing a deeper understanding of what drives business growth, and value creation.

What's Next

After advising businesses on complex transactions, I traded spreadsheets for a suitcase and spent a year traveling the world. Along the way, I gained new perspectives and reflected on what I wanted to pursue next. Now based in NYC, I'm exploring opportunities across finance, strategy, and business operations.

Off the Clock

Outside of work, you'll find me training for another triathlon, hiking somewhere with a view, trying new restaurants, enjoying live music with friends, 3D printing gadgets, or planning my next adventure.
